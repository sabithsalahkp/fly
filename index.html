GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

How do I enquire about an event? +

Fill out the enquiry form above or message us directly on WhatsApp. Include your event date, location and the kind of decor you're looking for.

Can I request a custom decoration theme? +

Absolutely. Share your reference images, colour palette or ideas through WhatsApp so we can discuss what's possible for your event.

Does submitting the form confirm my booking? +

No. The form only prepares a WhatsApp enquiry. Dates, services, prices and confirmation are discussed directly with the FLY team.

How will I receive pricing? +

Pricing depends on the location, event date, design and scope. Send your requirements and ask for a custom quote.
